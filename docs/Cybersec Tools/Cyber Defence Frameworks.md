# Cyber Defence Frameworks

This page has listed common Cyber defence frameworks briefly their contents.

## Cyber Kill Chain

Deveeloped by Lockheed Martin based on the military concept of kill chain. Encompasses the following phases:

- Reconnaissance
- Weaponization
- Delivery
- Exploitation
- Installation
- Command & Control
- Actions on Objectives 

**Reconnaissance** is discovering and collecting information on the system and the victim. The reconnaissance phase is the planning phase for the adversaries.

In the **Weaponization** phase, the attacker would choose the best tools and "weapons" for the attack.

The **Delivery** phase is when the attacker chooses the method for transmitting the payload or the malware. 

**Exploitaition** pahase contains the acttual running of the ewxploits.

**Installation** handles the installation of backdoors and detection evasion systems.

In the **Command & Control** phase the attacker opens up the C2 channel through the malware to remotely control and manipulate the victim.

**Actions on Objectives** refers to taking action on the original objectives of the attack.
